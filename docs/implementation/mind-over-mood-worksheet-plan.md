# Mind Over Mood worksheet coverage and update plan

Source: Dennis Greenberger and Christine A. Padesky, *Mind Over Mood*, second edition
(2016), supplied PDF, worksheet list on printed pp. xix-xx. This plan accounts for
all **60 numbered worksheets**. The IDs let editors compare the plan with the
book; the short topic labels below are editorial descriptions, not transcriptions
of the publisher's worksheets.

## Implementation update

The four proposed tools are now implemented as original, browser-local exercises: [Activity & Mood Log](../../site/tool-finder/activity-mood-log/index.qmd), [Core Belief Evidence Log](../../site/tool-finder/core-belief-evidence-log/index.qmd), [Anger Response Review](../../site/tool-finder/anger-response-review/index.qmd), and [Responsibility & Repair Planner](../../site/tool-finder/responsibility-repair/index.qmd). Existing goal, thought-record, gratitude, activation, beliefs, exposure, grounding, and maintenance experiences have targeted prompts or links. The inventories remain source references, without improvised scoring. The coverage table below retains the audit labels from before implementation so the rationale for each change stays visible.

Clinical-review status is separate from book attribution; these new prompts are not presented as individually approved by the reviewer.

## Scope and decisions

Build an original CBT curriculum using the current Thought Record, Five Factor
Model, Case Map, goal, exposure, gratitude, and planning tools. Treat a worksheet
as **Existing** only where a usable interactive tool already covers its main
practice. **Extend** means a tool exists but misses a material step. **Build**
means a new experience is needed. **Reference** means explain or link to the
publisher's material instead of creating a site version.

Four new tools are proposed:

1. **Activity & Mood Log**: brief entries over time, with activity, context,
   mood, and a reflection on enjoyment, connection, or accomplishment. Provide
   a low-effort view and links to Behavioural Activation. Do not imply a
   numerical mood change is required for an activity to be useful.
2. **Core Belief Evidence Log**: choose a flexible candidate belief, record
   specific observations over time, review contradictory or uncertain
   evidence, and note confidence without treating a score as proof. Link to
   the existing Beliefs Explorer and Behavioural Experiment Planner.
3. **Anger Response Review**: identify early cues, choose a pause or
   communication response, and review what helped. Link to STOP, DEAR MAN,
   Check the Facts, and Thought Record.
4. **Responsibility & Repair Planner**: distinguish what was under one's
   control from others' actions and circumstances, consider proportional
   responsibility, and choose whether any safe, appropriate repair is needed.
   Forgiveness and contact with another person must remain optional.

Extend existing tools where that gives a simpler experience:

- Thought Record: make facts versus interpretations and strengthening a new
  thought easier to practise; connect an actionable problem to goal planning.
- SMART Goal Builder: record the expected benefit and cost of change, practical
  support, and a personal sign of progress.
- Behavioural Activation: connect the current one-activity planner to the new
  multi-day log and its pattern review.
- Beliefs Explorer and Experiment Planner: link entries into the evidence log
  and make repeated observations easier to compare.
- Gratitude Journal: add reflection on what was noticed, plus optional
  gratitude-expression and kindness actions.
- Exposure and grounding: strengthen guidance and review fields only where the
  book exposes a concrete gap; retain the existing tools.
- Maintaining Progress Plan: make the skills review and personal warning signs
  easier to revisit, without copying the book's checklist.

The depression and anxiety inventories (13.1-13.2, 14.1-14.2) are
**Reference** items. They are specific measures with scoring conventions; do
not improvise equivalent scores or present them as diagnostic tools. Point
readers to the source book and appropriate professional support.

## Delivery order

1. Confirm the coverage calls below against the current rendered tool flows
   and record any corrections before writing new prompts. Draft original
   examples and source citations for each changed lesson and tool.
2. Build Activity & Mood Log and Core Belief Evidence Log. Integrate them with
   existing activation, beliefs, experiment, and thought-record pages.
3. Build Anger Response Review and Responsibility & Repair Planner. Review
   language carefully so uncertainty, coercion, abuse, and real-world safety
   are not reduced to thought errors or a duty to forgive.
4. Extend goals, gratitude, exposure, and maintenance where the audit below
   identifies specific missing practice. Add Tool Finder entries, Learn links,
   navigation, and genuinely useful glossary terms.
5. Validate source/generator consistency, tests, full Quarto render, and
   desktop/mobile browser flows. Check browser-local drafts, Markdown reopen,
   DOCX/PDF exports, older saved-file compatibility, keyboard labels, and
   absence of answer text in URLs. Have clinical-review status stated
   accurately; source citation alone does not mean clinical approval.

Keep the published prompts and examples independently written. Cite the 2016
edition by chapter and printed page, and do not publish scans, lightly
paraphrased worksheet forms, or the source PDF.

## Acceptance criteria for implementation

- Every row above has a live destination or a deliberate source reference,
  with no duplicate Tool Finder entry for the same exercise.
- New and extended tools have original wording, usable examples, chapter/page
  attribution, and consistent educational language.
- Entries stay in the browser unless the user saves/exports; worksheet text is
  absent from page URLs and network requests.
- Markdown save/reopen, DOCX export, PDF print, and older saved files work.
- Learn navigation and Tool Finder search find the new exercises by natural
  terms, including activity log, core belief, anger, responsibility, and
  Mind Over Mood.
- Keyboard labels and mobile layouts work without horizontal overflow.
- Relevant Python/Node tests, generated navigation/glossary checks, and the
  full Quarto render pass. Inspect representative rendered pages in a browser.
- The clinical reviewer is credited only for content actually reviewed by her.
