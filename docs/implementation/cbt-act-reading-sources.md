# CBT and ACT reading sources

The October 2026 additions use the uploaded books as editorial references. Full
book files and extracted text are local working material outside tracked site
content. No book PDFs, scanned pages, diagnostic measures, or publisher worksheets
were added to the public site by this change.

## Mind Over Mood

Dennis Greenberger and Christine A. Padesky, *Mind Over Mood*, second edition,
Guilford Press, copyright 2016. The supplied PDF has 367 physical pages. Printed
page 282 is PDF page 308. Worksheet 16.1 on printed pp. 282-285 was used to identify
coverage gaps; its table, rating system, and wording were not copied.

| New lesson | Reference | Existing tools reused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assumptions & Core Beliefs | Chapters 11-12, pp. 132-187 | Thought Record; Avoidance & Approach Planner |
| Working with Anger, Guilt & Shame | Chapter 15, pp. 252-279 | STOP; Check the Facts lesson; DEAR MAN; Thought Record |
| Maintaining Progress | Chapters 12-13 and 16; especially pp. 280-291 | Behavioural Activation; Gratitude Journal; Values Review; SMART Goal Builder |

The original six CBT lessons remain first in their established order. Three new
lessons follow them. These are educational summaries and original practice prompts,
not a complete adaptation of the book or replacement for its full exercises.

## Get Out of Your Mind and Into Your Life

Steven C. Hayes with Spencer Smith, *Get Out of Your Mind and Into Your Life*,
New Harbinger, 2005. The supplied PDF has 220 physical pages. Page references refer
to this edition; the publisher's product link now redirects to the anniversary
edition.

| New lesson | Reference |
| --- | --- |
| Making Room for Experience | Chapters 3-4 and 9-10 |
| Thoughts & the Observing Perspective | Chapters 5-8; especially pp. 69-104 |
| Values & Committed Action | Chapters 11-13, pp. 153-194 |

ACT has its own generated Learn navigation and sequence. The lessons connect to
existing Mindfulness, Grounding, Values, and planning tools. The other supplied
books, including *The Happiness Trap* and *The ACT Workbook for Depression and
Shame*, were inventoried but not bulk converted or published.

## Glossary references

The canonical editorial input remains `glossary/glossary.json`. Its 22 entries
include references from Linehan's second-edition mindfulness handouts, the APA
Dictionary of Psychology, the Beck Institute cognitive-model resource, and the
two books above. Definitions use concise original wording. Acronym search remains
case-insensitive; automatic definition links require uppercase acronyms so the
ordinary verb "act" does not link to ACT.

Generated navigation, catalogues, public glossary data, lookup data, and sequence
assets are produced by `python scripts/learn_glossary_site.py generate`. Tool Finder
topics are generated by `python scripts/tool_finder_topics.py generate`.
